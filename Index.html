Payment safety centre

Log a failed, pending, or unrecognised payment. This demo creates a local sample ticket only; it does not contact your bank or initiate a refund.

🛡️ Safety tip: Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone, including someone claiming to be support staff. A UPI PIN is used to authorise payments, not to receive money.
